What's next

  • Cut memory so 16 GB Macs play without swapping Chrome uses about 12.4 GB today, and Safari runs out of memory (tracked as RAY-104 to RAY-106)
  • Prepare the storm effect during loading an estimated 1.7 to 3.4 s off a first visit
  • Build far-away loot detail after the title an estimated 1.5 to 2.2 s off every visit
  • A new release can go out four times a day, at 10 AM, 1 PM, 4 PM and 7 PM Pacific, whenever there are changes waiting.

Waiting on people

  • Ray Decide whether the faster-title setting goes on by default: an earlier title screen with a less finished island behind it.
  • Ray Pick where the stable release is hosted.
  • Max 71 changes offered to the original game are waiting for review.
